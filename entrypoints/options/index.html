Local-First Architecture

Universal Website Performance Optimizer operates 100% locally within your browser. It does not collect browsing history, transmit telemetry, communicate with external servers, or store persistent tracking identifiers. All tab states are bound strictly to your temporary browsing session and are erased when tabs close.
